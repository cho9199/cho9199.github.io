What Photo Read does

Three things, in the order you need them.

1

Reads the text in a photo

Take a picture of the text you want to understand. The app recognises the characters for you — no typing, no cropping, no selection box.

2

Translates it into your language

Choose the language you want to read in. Text in any of the supported languages can be translated into the one you picked.

3

Speaks the result out loud

Tap confirm and, after a short ad, the translation is read to you with voice — useful when you would rather listen than squint.

4

Stays out of the way

No account, no setup, no manual. Large buttons and a simple screen so it works for parents and grandparents too.

Made for the moments you get stuck

Real situations Photo Read is built around.

  • Travelling — restaurant menus, subway and bus signs, notices at your hotel or guesthouse.
  • At the pharmacy or convenience store — ingredients lists, dosage instructions on a medicine packet or leaflet.
  • Small print — bills, forms and manuals printed too small to read comfortably.
  • Listening instead of reading — have a notice or document read out to you.
  • Foreign documents — product descriptions and paperwork you need to check.
  • Helping someone else — read a label or letter aloud for a parent or relative.

How it works

Four steps, about as long as it takes to open the camera.

  1. Choose a languagePick the language you want to read in.
  2. Take a photoFrame the text — a menu, a sign, a label — and shoot.
  3. Check the translationThe recognised text and the translation appear together.
  4. ListenPress confirm, watch a short ad, and the text is read aloud.

The languages

Free, with a short ad for the voice

Photo Read is free to install and free to use — there is no subscription and nothing to buy. The app is supported by ads, and one short ad unlocks the read-aloud voice for that photo. Everything else works without paying.

Reading, translating and copying the text out costs nothing; only the spoken version asks you to watch an ad.

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos you take and the text recognised from them are processed on your device and are not sent to a server. The app does not ask you to create an account, and no sign-in is required to use it.

The full policy, including how advertising works in the app, is here: Photo Read privacy policy.

Good to know

Accuracy

Blurry photos and handwriting can be recognised less accurately. A steady shot of printed text works best.

Voice data

On some devices you may need to install the voice data for a language in the system settings before it can be read aloud.

Android only

Photo Read is an Android app and is not available on iPhone or iPad.

Important information

For dosage, allergies and anything medical, check the original text alongside the translation rather than relying on the translation alone.

Contains ads. Photo Read is free because the app shows advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob; see the privacy policy for details.
