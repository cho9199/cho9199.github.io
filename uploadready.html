Photo Size Reducer

Compress photos to the exact KB you need

Ever hit “file is too large” when uploading a photo? Photo Size Reducer shrinks your photos to the KB size an application form asks for — 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB or any number you type — on your own phone, without sending them anywhere.

What it does

Four things, in the order you need them.

1

Compresses to the size you pick

Tap a preset — 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB — or type any target size. The app adjusts quality step by step until the photo fits.

2

Resizes to exact pixels

When a form asks for a specific width and height, enter the numbers and the photo is resized to those dimensions.

3

Handles many photos at once

Compress a batch in one go and see the size before and after, with the percentage you saved.

4

Converts the format

Save results as JPG, PNG or WebP, then keep them or share them straight away.

Made for the “file is too large” moment

Everyday situations the app is built around.

  • Job, school and visa applications — photo upload fields with a strict KB and pixel limit.
  • Online documents and ID photos — a passport-style photo or a scan that has to fit the upload box.
  • Selling online — product photos that are too heavy for a shop or marketplace listing.
  • Email and messengers — sending photos without huge attachments.
  • Website forms — sign-ups and applications that reject images over a certain size.

How it works

Four steps, about as long as it takes to pick a photo.

  1. Pick photosChoose one photo or several for a batch.
  2. Choose a sizeTap a preset or type a target KB, and the pixels if they matter.
  3. CompressThe app lowers quality and resolution step by step until the target is met.
  4. Save or shareResults are saved as new files; your originals stay untouched.

Free, with ads

Photo Size Reducer is free to install and free to use. There is no subscription and nothing to buy. The app is supported by ads, and a batch of more than five photos is unlocked by watching an ad.

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos are processed only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and the app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. Your original files are never overwritten — results are saved as new files — and location data (EXIF) is removed during conversion.

Good to know

Resolution can drop

To reach a very small target size, the app may reduce resolution as well as quality. The before-and-after sizes are shown so you can check the result.

Android only

Photo Size Reducer is an Android app (Android 7.0 and later). WebP conversion is available on Android.
